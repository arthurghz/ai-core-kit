<section className="ack-features">
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Archetype-first interview
    Pick your project shape first, then answer only the questions that apply.
    No LLM in the render loop — the same answers always produce the same repo.
  </div>
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Frozen manifest & render
    A single `project.manifest.yaml` is the source of truth, validated against a
    frozen JSON-Schema before the deterministic render engine stamps anything out.
  </div>
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Opt-in contract gate
    A conservative 3-mode design-contract gate ships as a hook into the child —
    enforced where you want it, never imposed where you don't.
  </div>
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Offline cost telemetry + Grafana
    Hooks can't read token cost live, so an offline aggregator joins transcripts
    against a versioned pricing map for per-feature, per-model, per-agent spend.
  </div>
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Harvested skills & agents
    A curated catalog of `.claude/` skills, RPI agents, and commands across the
    META and CHILD layers — wired deterministically by your manifest.
  </div>
  <div className="ack-card">
    ### Design system + shadcn
    Fullstack projects can opt into a design-system payload: shadcn/ui, brand
    guidelines, an MCP for component discovery, and theme tokens.
  </div>
</section>
