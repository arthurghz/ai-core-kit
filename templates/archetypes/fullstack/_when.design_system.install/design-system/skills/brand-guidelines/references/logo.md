# Logo usage

Rules for placing the brand logo. A fork supplies its actual assets and may
tighten these; the structure is the part to keep.

## Asset inventory

Maintain, at minimum:

- **Primary lockup** — wordmark + symbol, full color.
- **Symbol only** — the mark alone, for avatars/favicons/tight spaces.
- **Monochrome** — single-color versions for light and dark backgrounds.
- **Formats** — SVG for screen (scales cleanly); PNG fallbacks at 1x/2x where
  SVG is not accepted.

## Clear space

Keep a protected margin around the logo free of other elements. Define it
relative to the mark (e.g. the height of the symbol, or the cap-height of the
wordmark) so it scales with the logo. Default: clear space equal to the symbol
height on all sides.

## Minimum size

Set a minimum rendered size below which the logo must not appear, to preserve
legibility:

- Primary lockup: not smaller than ~120px wide on screen.
- Symbol only: not smaller than ~24px.

Below the lockup minimum, switch to the symbol-only asset.

## Color treatments

- On light backgrounds: full-color primary lockup.
- On dark backgrounds: the dark-background monochrome or the approved reversed
  lockup — never the light-background version dropped onto dark.
- On photography or busy backgrounds: use a solid container or the monochrome
  version to keep contrast; the logo must meet the 3:1 graphical-object contrast
  minimum against its background.

## Prohibited alterations

Do not:

- Stretch, condense, or otherwise distort the proportions.
- Recolor outside the approved color set.
- Add drop shadows, glows, gradients, or strokes.
- Rotate, skew, or apply perspective.
- Rearrange or resize the lockup's parts relative to each other.
- Place the logo on a low-contrast background that fails the contrast minimum.
- Reconstruct the logo from the brand font — always use the supplied asset.

When in doubt, use the primary lockup at a generous size on a clean,
high-contrast background.
