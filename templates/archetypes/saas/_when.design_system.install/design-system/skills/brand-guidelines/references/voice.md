# Voice and tone

Voice is the brand's consistent personality across all copy. Tone is how that
voice flexes for context — celebratory on success, calm and concrete on error.
Voice is part of the brand identity; treat it with the same rigor as color.

## Voice profile (defaults)

A fork should rewrite these to match its brand, but the defaults are a safe
baseline:

- **Clear over clever.** Say the thing plainly. Skip wordplay that adds reading
  cost.
- **Direct, second person.** Address the reader as "you"; use active voice.
- **Confident, not hyped.** State what the product does; avoid superlatives and
  marketing inflation ("revolutionary", "seamless", "blazing-fast").
- **Respectful of attention.** Short sentences. Front-load the point. Cut
  filler.
- **Human, not robotic.** Contractions are fine; jargon is not.

## Tone by context

| Context            | Tone                                                   |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| Onboarding / empty | Warm, encouraging, action-oriented                     |
| Success            | Brief, affirming — confirm and get out of the way      |
| Error              | Calm and concrete: what happened + what to do next     |
| Destructive confirm| Plain and serious; name the consequence exactly        |
| Marketing          | More expressive, but still grounded — never hype        |

## Mechanics

- **Sentence case** for UI labels, buttons, and headings (not Title Case, not
  ALL CAPS).
- **Buttons are verbs**: "Save changes", "Delete project" — not "OK"/"Submit".
- **Numbers and dates** follow the product locale; be consistent.
- **No dead ends.** Every error and empty state offers a next step.

## Word list

| Prefer            | Avoid                                  |
|-------------------|----------------------------------------|
| "Sign in"         | "Login" (as a verb)                    |
| "Delete"          | "Remove"/"Trash" used interchangeably  |
| "Something went wrong — try again" | "Error 500" / "Oops!" |
| plain feature names | "leverage", "synergy", "utilize"     |

## Before / after

> Before: "Oops! An unexpected error occurred while attempting to process your
> request. Please try again later."
>
> After: "We couldn't save your changes. Check your connection and try again."

> Before: "Your blazing-fast, next-gen dashboard is ready!"
>
> After: "Your dashboard is ready."

Apply the voice profile and the appropriate tone to every string the product
shows — labels, hints, errors, emails, and empty states alike.
